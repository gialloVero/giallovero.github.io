Computronium - [Grey Matter Games]

My Internship at Grey Matter Games


Project Goal

Computronium is an automation game based on building and managing a retro sci-fi datacenter.
Currently in development.
It's designed to grant flexible multiplayer and full mod support to try to strive for a community first approach for both self-advertisement and user-created content.

Grey Matter Games is a small emerging company founded by industry veterans with a great interest for open-source development and a focus on longer development cycles.

You can find out more about the game on the official Computronium Steam Page.

My Contributions

I joined the company as an intern during the Spring 2025 semester.
In my intership, I embraced a generalist butterfly role, working across various fields and task, both trying to learn as much as possible, and to give back to the project my own contribution.
